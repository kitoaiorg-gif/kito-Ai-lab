 KITO AI LAB - Kito AI Platform
 AI-powered health triage via USSD, SMS & WhatsApp - Offline-first for Uganda

 Overview
KITO AI LAB is a youth-led Ugandan startup using AI to improve access to healthcare and fight antimicrobial resistance (AMR).

Across Uganda and East Africa, many self-medicate because healthcare is expensive and facilities are far. ~75% buy antibiotics OTC without prescription, fueling Africa's drug-resistant infections.

Kito AI provides health guidance via USSD (123#) and SMS on basic phones (no smartphone/data/internet needed) and WhatsApp, in Luganda, Swahili & English. We do not prescribe - we triage and refer to clinics, labs & accredited pharmacies.

Built offline-first for USSD session limits, informed by Ugandan medicines information & National Drug Authority (NDA) guidance, with multilingual NLP.

Traction: 17,700 unique individuals served. 68% Luganda, 45% referred to formal care, 70% completed follow-up.

Status: Accepted by Villgro Africa Core Incubation.
